SCHEME: Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)

OVERVIEW:
PMAY-G provides financial assistance to rural households to construct pucca (permanent) houses. The central government provides Rs. 1.20 lakh in plain areas and Rs. 1.30 lakh in hilly/difficult areas.

ELIGIBILITY:
- Families listed in SECC 2011 data as houseless or living in 0/1 room kutcha houses
- Priority to SC/ST, minorities, widows, disabled persons
- Must not have any pucca house anywhere in India
- Family income should be below poverty line

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar card (all family members)
- Bank account (linked to Aadhaar)
- MGNREGA job card (if applicable)
- Land ownership documents

FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE:
- Rs. 1.20 lakh (plain areas) / Rs. 1.30 lakh (hilly/NE/difficult areas)
- Additional Rs. 12,000 for toilet construction under Swachh Bharat Mission
- Rs. 18,000 for 90 days of unskilled labour under MGNREGA
- Total support can reach up to Rs. 1.50 lakh

HOW TO APPLY:
1. Check eligibility on AwaasSoft portal or at Gram Panchayat
2. Village Panchayat maintains the list of eligible beneficiaries
3. Contact Block Development Officer (BDO) or Gram Pradhan
4. Application is processed through Gram Sabha
