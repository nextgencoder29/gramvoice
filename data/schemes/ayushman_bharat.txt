SCHEME: Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)

OVERVIEW:
Ayushman Bharat provides health insurance coverage of Rs. 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalization. It is the world's largest health assurance scheme targeting poor and vulnerable families.

ELIGIBILITY:
Based on SECC (Socio-Economic Caste Census) 2011 data:
- Families living in kutcha houses
- Families with no adult member between 16-59 years
- Households with disabled members
- SC/ST households
- Landless agricultural labor households

HOW TO CHECK ELIGIBILITY:
- Visit mera.pmjay.gov.in or call 14555
- Check at nearest Ayushman Mitra or government hospital
- Visit Common Service Centre with Aadhaar card

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar card
- Ration card
- Mobile number

HOW TO USE:
1. Visit any empanelled government or private hospital
2. Show Aadhaar card at Ayushman Mitra desk
3. Hospital will verify your eligibility online
4. Treatment is FREE — no cash payment needed

WHAT IS COVERED:
- Surgery, medical and daycare treatments
- Pre and post-hospitalization
- Medicines, diagnostics, ICU services
- Over 1,500 treatment packages

HELPLINE: 14555 (toll-free, 24x7)
